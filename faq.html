A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

A few useful things to know before you choose your window coverings.

Where can I see fabric samples?+

Visit our Edmonton showroom at 9790 51 Ave NW. Seeing samples in person is the best way to compare colour, texture and the way a fabric filters light.

What is the difference between dual and roller shades?+

Dual shades use alternating sheer and solid bands that shift against each other. Roller shades use one continuous fabric that raises or lowers. Your preferred appearance and the amount of light you want are good starting points.

Will blackout fabric make my room completely dark?+

Blackout describes the fabric. Light can still enter around the sides or top of a shade. Discuss the window, mounting position and your room-darkening expectations with us before choosing.

Can my shades be motorized?+

Motor options depend on the product, dimensions and chosen system. Tell us which windows you have in mind and how you want to control them so we can confirm a suitable option.

Do I need exact measurements before getting in touch?+

No. Approximate sizes, a window count and a few photos are useful for an initial conversation. Confirm the final measuring process with our team before placing your order.

How much do custom blinds cost?+

Your quote depends on the window sizes, fabric, controls and installation requirements. Contact us for a free consultation and a quote based on your project.

How long will my order take?+

Timing varies with the products and project. Ask our team to confirm the current lead time with your quote.

Which areas do you serve?+

We welcome enquiries from Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove, Leduc and surrounding communities. Contact us to check arrangements for your location.

What warranty applies to my order?+

Ask for the warranty details for your selected products and motors with your quote. Coverage and service terms should be confirmed for the specific items you order.
