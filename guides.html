THE SHADE GUIDE

Start with the room.
Then find the shade.

You don’t have to know the product names yet. These four simple questions will help you find a direction.

01

How do you use the room?

A bedroom, a busy kitchen and a sunny office each ask something different of a window covering. Start with what you do in the space and when you spend time there.

02

What should the light feel like?

Do you want a soft glow or a more restful setting? Compare light filtering, sunscreen and blackout fabrics in person. Remember that the fit around the window matters too.

03

What does privacy mean here?

Think about the view from outside, both during the day and when your lights are on at night. Talk through those conditions when choosing a fabric.

04

How do you want it to work?

Consider access to the window, who uses the room and whether motorized control would make the everyday easier. Ask us to confirm suitable options for your window sizes.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A little preparation.
A useful conversation.

  • Bring photos of the room and windows.
  • Note approximate sizes and the number of windows.
  • Collect a few colours or finishes you love.
  • Think about privacy, glare and daily routines.

MEASURING & INSTALLATION

A good fit starts
with good information.

A few details help us prepare your consultation. Final order measurements should be confirmed with our team.

01 / PHOTOGRAPH

Show the whole window.

Take a straight-on photo including the frame. Note handles, trim or anything that might affect how a shade fits.

02 / MAKE A NOTE

Label each room.

Record the window count and approximate width and height, including the units. These are a starting point for a discussion, not final manufacturing sizes.

03 / CONFIRM THE FIT

Let us measure.

Book a free measurement visit. Ask us to confirm the mounting position, light gaps, controls and installation arrangements before ordering.
