THE WINDOW JOURNAL

Small details.
A different feeling.

A few thoughtful ideas for living with light.

01 / LIVING WITH LIGHT

Notice the light before you choose.

Spend a day watching your room. A window that feels gentle at breakfast might catch strong afternoon light. Make a note of the moments when you reach for shade, move your chair or switch on a lamp.

Those small observations are a useful starting point for a conversation about fabric. Bring them along when you visit the showroom.

Explore light filtering fabrics↗

02 / A COHESIVE HOME

Related, without being identical.

Your rooms can feel connected without every window wearing exactly the same shade. Try a consistent colour family while choosing different levels of light control for the way each room is used.

Lay samples beside flooring, paint and furniture finishes. Look at them together in daylight before deciding.

Compare the collections↗

03 / MAKING A CHOICE

The value of seeing it in person.

A photo is a starting point. A real sample shows you the weave, texture and small shifts in colour that a screen can miss.

At the showroom, compare a few options side by side. Ask to see how the fabric changes when light passes through it, and talk about how it will sit in your window.
